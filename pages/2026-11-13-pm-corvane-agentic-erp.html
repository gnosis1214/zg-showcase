Why we built it

Most ERPs are a very expensive memory. They record what your people did, days after they did it. Corvane does the work: agents that read, decide and post, inside the limits your controllers set, with every step on the record.

Architecture

Five layers. One record.

Agents never write around the ledger or around your rules. Every layer only talks to the one next to it.

PEOPLE
GUARDRAILS
AGENT MESH
LEDGER CORE
CONNECTORS

  1. Your people

    Approvers, policy owners and a weekly digest. The final say stays human.

  2. Guardrails

    Limits, roles and segregation of duties, checked before every single action.

  3. Agent mesh

    Forty specialised agents and an orchestrator that hands work between them.

  4. Ledger core

    Double-entry and append-only. One source of truth that every agent reads from.

  5. Connectors

    Read and write to the systems you run today. Nothing gets ripped out on day one.
