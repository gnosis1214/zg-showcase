The composition

Top notes · the first 15 minutes

Sea salt and bergamot

The first spray is bright and a little briny, like wind coming off the water. Calabrian bergamot keeps it sparkling while a mineral salt accord dries it down fast.

  • Calabrian bergamot
  • Sea salt accord
  • Pink pepper

Heart notes · hours one to four

Night jasmine and fig leaf

As the citrus fades, jasmine sambac opens up: creamy, a touch green, never sweet. Fig leaf adds the cool shade of a garden wall after sunset.

  • Jasmine sambac
  • Fig leaf
  • Orris butter

Base notes · the long dry-down

Amber and vetiver

What stays is warm and quiet. An ambergris accord and Haitian vetiver sit close to skin, a little salty still, like sun-warmed driftwood.

  • Ambergris accord
  • Haitian vetiver
  • Ambrette seed
